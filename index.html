Snowflake | Streamlit | Iceberg | Nov 2026

Month-End Close Reconciliation

Problem
Payroll, the general ledger and the bank record the same money in different formats, so a missed entry is easy to overlook.
What I did
Loaded three raw exports into Iceberg tables, cleaned them into one ledger of 46 transactions, matched every bank payment to the ledger, and built a Streamlit dashboard with a payroll tie-out, a Semantic View and a Cortex Agent.
Result
Found two unreconciled bank transactions worth $47,200 and tied payroll to the ledger on all three pay dates.
Built in the Snowflake Northstar Expedition 2026 lab
